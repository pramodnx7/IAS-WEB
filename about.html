About IEEE IAS Sri Lanka Chapter

Empowering engineering professionals and driving industrial innovation across Sri Lanka through a robust network of expertise and collaboration.

M

Our Mission

To advance the theory and practice of electrical and electronic engineering in the development, design, manufacture, and application of electrical systems across industry and society.

V

Our Vision

To become Sri Lanka's most active professional platform for industry applications, enabling engineers to learn, lead, collaborate, and create measurable impact.
