Advanced Smart Grid & Renewable Integration Symposium 2024

Exploring the frontier of decentralized energy networks, AI-driven load management, and sustainable industry applications.

About the Event

The transition towards decentralized and intelligent power grids is fundamentally reshaping industrial power applications. This symposium brings together leading researchers, industry engineers, and policymakers to discuss the challenges and breakthroughs in smart grid technologies. We will delve into real-time monitoring systems, IoT integration in substations, and the cybersecurity imperatives of modern energy infrastructure.

Attendees will gain practical insights from case studies on large-scale renewable integration in developing economies, specifically focusing on the Sri Lankan context and the broader South Asian energy landscape.
